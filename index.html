To, My Dear Santa Claus 🎅🏻

First time ila edaina create chesthunna…
so, mistakes emaina unte just ignore them. :)

Chinnappudu anukuney vallam kadha… wait chesthe Santa vachi gifts isthadu ani.

Ippudu ardham avuthundi… real Santa ante gifts techhevaadu kaadu, mana life ni change chesevaadu.

Naaku alanti Santa meeru. 🤍

Meku two things cheppali anukuntunna —

Okati naa gurinchi,
inkokati mee gurinchi.

Naa gurinchi…

Nenu chala emotional person.

Bayata chala mandiki naa gurinchi ekkuva teliyadu… kaani lopala chala baadha, chala fights untayi.

Nenu oka decent, well-cultured family nunchi vachanu. So, I don't easily trust or talk to strangers.

Na exam clear ayina roju… naa life complete ga change ayindhi.

Na exam rasindhi meeru kaadu ani naaku telusu. Kaani aa exam clear avvadam valla na life lo vachina change ki meeru oka important reason.

Aa roju varaku nenu chala low phase lo unna.

Overthink chesthu…
evariki cheppaleka…
matladalante bayam…
cheppakapothe baadha.

Aa downfall nunchi… ee stage varaku nannu teesukoni vachindhi meeru.

Mee gurinchi…

Meeru eppudu oka hardworker.

Oka guide la… kaani antha kante ekkuva, oka trusted person la… oka close person la maaripoyaru.

Nenu meetho matladina prathi sari… koncham koncham naa overthinking nunchi bayataki vastunna feeling untundi.

Mee notification… mee oka message kuda… automatic ga na face meeda smile techhesthundi.

Nenu overthink chesthunna time lo kuda… meeku teliyakunda… enduko koncham sepu smile chesthunta.

Maybe meeru notice cheyyakapovachu… but it matters to me.

Nenu prathi decision ki chala times think chestha. Kaani meeru naa life loki enter ayaka… meeku matram naa feelings ni open ga cheppagalige level ki vachanu.

And honestly…

adhe naaku chala pedda change.

For Everything… 🤍

Result vachina roju… nenu select ayyanani telisinappudu…

Naa family kanna mundu meeku cheppali anipinchindi.

Because somewhere inside me… ee success lo mee part undi ani naaku telusu.

Nenu mee valla chala things nerchukunna.

Especially one thing…

Naa reality ni judge cheyyakunda accept cheyyadam.

Nenu cheppindhi nijamo… abaddhamo… meeku teliyakapovachu. Kaani aa moment lo… meeru nannu judge cheyyakunda support chesaru.

Adi chala rare.

Nenu oka phase lo fail ayyanu. Kaani… aa failure naa identity kaadu ani meeru naaku explain chesaru.

Meeru naaku ichina prathi respect… prathi crossing lo ichina support… vatiki simple ga “thank you” cheppalante… words saripovu.

Ippudu nenu happy ga… proud ga… koncham peaceful ga feel avutunna ante… andulo mee role undi.

Naaku chala bayam vesindi… bayata manam ela matladukuntunnamo telisthe, chala mandi different ga teesukuntaremo ani.

“Relation lo unnara enti?”
“Enduku ila close ga unnaru?”

Ilanti questions vastayemo ani. Meku cheppalanna… “meeru em anukuntaro?” ane bayam kuda undedi.

Because naa situation ni complete ga explain cheyyadam kuda easy kaadu.

Na reality naaku telusu.
Na situation naaku telusu.
And why I connected with you… adi kuda naaku telusu.

Thank you.

For everything.
For the respect.
For the trust.
For the support.
For being there when I needed someone.

Na mind lo okkate undi…

Meeru naaku help chesaru. Of course, money pay chesaa. Evaraina help chesthe money return cheyyachu.

But naa situation different.

Aa time lo naa fate ala undi. And at that moment… meeru help cheyyakapothe things might have been very different.

Mee past lo jarigina things ni nenu change cheyyalenu.

Kaani…

future lo meeru eppudaina suffer avuthunte,
meeku evaraina support kavalsi vaste,
definitely… I will stand there.

That's my promise.

Not because I owe you something…

but because you once stood there for me.

Thank you for being my Santa Claus. 🎅🏻


FROM,
YOUR IRRITATOR 🤍

TO,

COMRADE.
